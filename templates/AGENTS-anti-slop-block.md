## Writing style (docs, commits, PRs)

- Start with the fact. No warm-up line, no `Certainly!`.
- Plain words. `use`, not `leverage`. No hype (`blazing fast`, `seamless`, `game-changer`).
- No `It's not X, it's Y`. No em dashes. No emoji at the start of list items or headings.
- Commit subjects: imperative, under 72 characters, no period.
- PR text: What, Why, How to test, Risk. Only claim tests you saw pass.
- Before you finish, reread your text against these rules.
